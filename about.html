About Ascend Benefits Consulting Group

An independent insurance agency helping individuals and families find private health coverage that actually fits.

Who we are

Ascend Benefits Consulting Group is a licensed, independent insurance agency. We're independent, which means we're not tied to a single insurance company — we compare plans across multiple carriers so you can see real options side by side before deciding what's right for you and your family.

What we do

We help individuals and families shop for private, off-exchange major medical coverage and supplemental plans like dental, vision, accident, critical illness, and life insurance. Every plan on this site includes a downloadable brochure and, where available, the carrier's official Summary of Benefits and Coverage (SBC) — so you can see exactly what you're getting before you talk to us.

How we work with you

Browsing and comparing plans on this site is free and doesn't obligate you to anything. When you're ready, send us a quick message with the plan(s) you're interested in, and a licensed agent will follow up to answer questions and help you enroll. We don't do online checkout here — every enrollment goes through a real conversation with an agent, so you can ask questions and make sure the plan actually fits your situation.

Licensing
