Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

What we collect

When you use the "Talk to an Agent" form on this site, we collect the information you enter: your full name, email address, phone number, state, the plan(s) you're interested in, and any message you include. We don't require an account, and browsing or comparing plans on this site doesn't require you to submit any personal information.

How we use it

We use the information you submit to respond to your inquiry, answer your questions, and help you compare and enroll in coverage if you choose to. If you move forward with a plan, we may share the relevant information with the insurance carrier as part of the application and enrollment process — that's a normal part of how insurance applications work and isn't optional if you want to enroll.

What we don't do

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We don't use your contact information for anything beyond responding to your inquiry and, if you choose to move forward, helping you apply for coverage.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time what information we have about you, request that we delete it, or ask us to stop contacting you. Reach out using the contact information below and we'll take care of it.

Questions

If you have questions about this policy or how your information is handled, contact us at info@ascendbenefitscg.com.
